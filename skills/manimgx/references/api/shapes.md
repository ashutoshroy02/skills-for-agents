# Shapes

A shape is a path: curves that a stroke draws and a fill fills (see [Paths](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/reference/mobjects/paths/index.md>)). Each kind of shape is a class: make one with its size, then place and style it as any mobject.

- [**Lines and arrows**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/reference/mobjects/shapes/lines-and-arrows/index.md>)

  ---

  Lines between points, arrows that point, vectors, and the tips that end them.
- [**Circles and arcs**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/reference/mobjects/shapes/circles-and-arcs/index.md>)

  ---

  Circles, dots, ellipses and arcs, and the rings and slices made from them.
- [**Polygons**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/reference/mobjects/shapes/polygons/index.md>)

  ---

  Shapes of straight sides, from triangles to stars, and the rectangles that frame the screen.
- [**Combining shapes**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/reference/mobjects/shapes/combining-shapes/index.md>)

  ---

  A shape's union, intersection or difference with another, holes, and hulls.
