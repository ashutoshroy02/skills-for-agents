# Gallery

Forty-eight short films made with ManimGX, each one Python file that needs only numpy. Every number on screen is computed as the film plays.

## Equations and proofs

- [**The quadratic formula**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/gallery/quadratic-formula/index.md>)

  Completing the square turns an equation into a picture.
- [**Pythagoras by rearrangement**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/gallery/pythagoras/index.md>)

  Moving four right triangles reveals why a² + b² = c².
- [**Unrolling a circle**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/gallery/circle-area/index.md>)

  Thin rings unroll into strips that stack into a triangle.
- [**Euler's identity as a walk**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/gallery/euler-identity/index.md>)

  The terms of the exponential series spiral toward −1 in the complex plane.

## Calculus

- [**What a derivative measures**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/gallery/derivative/index.md>)

  A secant becomes a tangent as two points on a curve come together.
- [**Rectangles become an integral**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/gallery/riemann-sums/index.md>)

- [**Polynomials become the sine**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/gallery/taylor-series/index.md>)

  Each new Taylor term makes a polynomial follow more of the sine wave.
- [**A circle draws sine and cosine**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/gallery/unit-circle/index.md>)

  A rotating radius traces sine and cosine from its height and reach.

## Patterns and surprises

- [**Circles draw π**](<https://manimgx.academa.ai/gallery/fourier-pi/index.md>)
